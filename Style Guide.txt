Lighten 1 step: +5val to R,G,B
Darken 1 step: -5val to R,G,B
(make sure to note how many steps Lightend / Darkened as a comment:
Written <Brightness:N*5>
where N is how many steps, if darkened, write N as a negative number EG:
<Brightness:-4*5> = -20)
````
